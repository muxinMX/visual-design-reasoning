# Optional Visual Analysis Template

Use only the sections that improve the requested analysis. Do not force a simple observation into a full report.

## Subject and hierarchy

- Main subject or information focus
- Supporting elements and visual order

## Form and proportion

- Silhouette, geometry, scale, proportion, and recognition cues

## Visual style

- Abstraction level, line or edge behavior, surface treatment, and detail density

## Color

- Dominant and supporting colors, value grouping, saturation, and temperature relationships

## Composition and camera

- Framing, viewpoint, depth, perspective, negative space, and visual path

## Material and lighting

- Material response, light direction and softness, contrast, reflections, and atmosphere

## Transferable principles

- Visual rules that can be reused independently of the depicted content

## Uncertainty

- Estimates, interpretations, and information that cannot be verified from the reference alone
