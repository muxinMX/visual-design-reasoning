# Optional Video Prompt Template

Use for image-to-video or animation tasks that benefit from explicit continuity and timing. Omit fields that do not matter.

- **Initial state:** character or object pose, environment, camera, and motion readiness
- **Core action:** one readable action goal
- **Timeline:** anticipation, initiation, main action, follow-through, and final hold
- **Physical behavior:** center of mass, support, contact, force, inertia, arcs, and secondary motion
- **Camera behavior:** static, tracking, pan, tilt, push, pull, orbit, or another specified movement
- **Expression and gaze:** changes synchronized with the action
- **End state:** final pose, framing, and settled motion
- **Continuity constraints:** identity, proportions, clothing or components, environment, lighting, and camera consistency
- **Avoid:** morphing, identity drift, duplicated limbs or parts, sliding contact, background instability, and unrequested camera changes
